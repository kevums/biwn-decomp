The private C frame ends below both original public saves. The checks exclude its private 8 KB stack region below those saves, and the candidate code/entry overlay. They do not prove IRQ/fault/cycle equivalence, arbitrary stack/code/counter aliasing, concurrent accesses, actual game callers, disc integration, or physical hardware behavior. Ordered comparisons use Unicorn; returned CPU/RAM comparisons also execute the original through the retained native CPU core. These are scoped emulator tests, not a formal proof of the whole ISA or game.
